About Us

Red Bull is a globally recognized energy drink that essentially pioneered the modern energy drink category upon its launch in Austria on 1 April 1987. The brand was co-founded by Austrian entrepreneur Dietrich Mateschitz and Thai businessman Chaleo Yoovidhya, who adapted a Thai tonic called Krating Daeng to suit Western tastes by carbonating it and refining the flavor. Known for its iconic blue-and-silver slim cans and the famous slogan "Red Bull gives you wiiings," the beverage contains a blend of caffeine, taurine, B-group vitamins, and sugars designed to boost mental and physical performance.

Beyond the drink itself, Red Bull has built a massive cultural presence through unconventional marketing, moving away from traditional ads to focus on extreme sports, athlete sponsorships, and high-octane events like the Red Bull Flugtag and Formula 1. Today, it remains the world's most-consumed energy drink, with over 13.9 billion cans sold in 2025 alone and a presence in 178 countries Red Bull’s approach to customer service is unique because it focuses on the user experience and brand lifestyle rather than traditional retail.
